# FOB vs CIF vs EXW for Small Importers

Incoterms define who pays for and handles shipping steps.

- **EXW (Ex Works):** you collect from the factory and handle almost everything. Maximum control, maximum responsibility.
- **FOB (Free On Board):** the seller delivers the goods onto the vessel at the named port; you handle the main freight onward.
- **CIF (Cost, Insurance and Freight):** the seller pays freight and minimum insurance to your destination port.

## Which to choose?
Beginners often prefer a quote including freight to their port, while experienced importers with a forwarder prefer FOB. Always specify the exact port and the Incoterms 2020 version in the contract. Ask a customs broker or forwarder to confirm.
